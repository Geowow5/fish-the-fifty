# Fish the Fifty — Great Salt Plains Lake update

This update adds a new Oklahoma water guide at `/states/oklahoma/great-salt-plains` and links it from the Oklahoma state page.

## Included

- Great Salt Plains Lake guide with Coon Hollow, north spillway, and seasonal refuge shoreline access notes
- Official ODWC, U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service, U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, and Oklahoma lake-map links
- Current source-linked seasonal refuge guidance and the area-specific saugeye limit
- General starting tactics, a fly-fishing note, map searches, and trip-planning links

## Upload

Upload the ZIP contents to the repository root, preserving the `app/`, `public/`, `package.json`, `package-lock.json`, `tsconfig.json`, and `next-env.d.ts` paths. Do not upload the ZIP as a nested file or upload `.next/` and `node_modules/`.
